The Practice

Three areas. One connected picture.

Most problems in a business get treated one at a time. Growth is chased without checking margin. Cost is cut until the business cannot deliver. I look at all three together, because a change in one always moves the other two.

01 Revenue

Business Growth

Find the demand you are already close to, then build a repeatable way to win it. Growth that depends on one person having a good month is not growth.

  • Where the money really comes from: customers, products, channels, margin by segment
  • Pricing and packaging that match the value you deliver
  • A sales process that still works when the owner is not in the room
  • Entering a new segment or market through a small, testable first step
  • Keeping the customers you have: retention, repeat, and win-back
02 Efficiency

Profit Maximization

Same revenue, more of it left at the bottom. In most businesses the profit is already there, sitting inside cost, waste, rework and slow cash.

  • Cost base read line by line, split into what creates value and what does not
  • Process redesign where the delays, handovers and rework actually live
  • Working capital: inventory, receivables, payment terms, cash cycle
  • Automation for the work your team repeats every single week
  • Buying better: suppliers, contracts, and what you should stop paying for
03 Governance

Business Resilience

A business that survives a bad quarter, a key person leaving, or an audit. Resilience is not paperwork. It is knowing who decides what, on what information, and by when.

  • Decision rights: who decides what, and with which numbers in front of them
  • A management routine: the report, the meeting, the follow-through
  • Risk register and controls sized to your business, not a corporate template
  • Documented processes and cover for roles only one person can do
  • Cash and scenario planning, so a shock is a plan and not a surprise

The Method

Diagnose. Prioritise. Build. Hand over.

The same four steps every time, whatever the size of the business. Each step ends with something you can hold: a written finding, an agreed list, a working change, a routine your team owns.

01

Diagnose

Two to three weeks. I read the numbers, sit with the team, and walk the process from first contact to cash in the bank. You get a written picture of the business as it runs, not as the org chart describes it.

02

Prioritise

We rank every opportunity by cash impact against effort and risk, then agree a short list. Most businesses have twenty good options and the capacity to do four of them properly.

03

Build

We implement together, in short cycles, each one with a result you can measure. I work inside the business alongside your people. The output is a change that runs, not a deck that sits in a drawer.

04

Hand over

The routine, the numbers and a named owner for each change stay with your team. We agree what good looks like in ninety days. Success is you not needing me.
